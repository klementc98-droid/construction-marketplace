{% load i18n %}{% comment %} StepProgress — where you are in a sequence of questions. Two things, both needed. The bar answers "how much longer" at a glance, which is what decides whether somebody starts at all. The words answer it precisely, which is what stops the bar being read as a loading spinner. It renders hidden and the script shows it, because with no script the whole form is on one screen: a progress bar on a page with no steps is a lie about a sequence that is not happening. The sentence is handed to the script as a pattern with {n} and {of} in it, rather than rebuilt from pieces in JavaScript. "Step 1 of 6" is one sentence in one catalogue entry, and a language that words it differently — or puts the numbers elsewhere — still gets to say it its own way. {% endcomment %} {% blocktranslate asvar step_pattern with n="{n}" of="{of}" %}Step {{ n }} of {{ of }}{% endblocktranslate %}
