The whole thing is inert until pressed — assistant.js does nothing on load beyond binding the launcher, so a page nobody asks for help on pays nothing for this being here. There used to be a choice here: help me fill in a form, or answer a question. The form half is gone. Posting a job and writing a profile now ask one question per screen on the form itself, where an answer is typed into the box it belongs to and can be seen beside the others — a chat that collects the same values was a slower route to a screen you had to look at anyway. So the panel opens straight into the conversation. A menu with one item on it is a menu that exists to be got past. {% endcomment %}
