{% translate "What we do" %}

{% translate "Construction's Finest is a hiring board for the building trades. Day gigs and standing positions, in one market, with the money for a gig held before anyone turns up." %}

{% translate "The trades already had places to find work. What they did not have was any answer to the question every worker asks before a first job with a stranger:" %} {% translate "am I actually going to get paid?" %} {% translate "Chasing an invoice for a day's work costs more in time than the day was worth, so most people stop chasing — and the client who never pays finds someone new next week." %}

{% translate "So we built the board around the money rather than bolting payment on at the end. For a dated gig, the client's card is charged before the worker travels. Nobody works on a promise." %}

{% translate "The two kinds of post" %}

{% translate "A gig" %} {% translate "is one dated shift at a fixed price — a day, a price, a site. This is the kind that runs through escrow, because it has a defined end that everyone can agree has happened." %}

{% translate "A standing position" %} {% translate "is an open role paid at a rate rather than a total: full-time, part-time, ongoing. We introduce the two sides and step out of it. There is no escrow here, because there is no single day to sign off." %}

{% translate "How the money moves on a gig" %}

  • {% translate "The client funds the job" %}
    The full advertised price is authorised on their card — held, not yet taken. Funding opens up to {{ funding_window_days }} day{{ funding_window_days|pluralize }} before the gig; card authorisations expire, so a hold placed weeks out would be dead by the morning of the job.
  • {% translate "The worker checks in on site" %}
    From check-in, they are guaranteed at least {{ minimum_hours|floatformat:"-1" }} hour{{ minimum_hours|floatformat:"-1"|pluralize }} of pay however early the day ends — someone who travelled to a site and was sent home after twenty minutes has still spent their morning.
  • {% translate "The work is signed off" %}
    {% blocktranslate %}The client has {{ approval_hours }} hours to approve or dispute. If they say nothing, the money releases anyway. Silence is approval here on purpose: an unresponsive client must not be able to strand a worker's pay by simply not opening the app.{% endblocktranslate %}
  • {% translate "The worker is paid" %}
    Straight to their bank via Stripe. We take {{ fee_pct|floatformat:"-2" }}%, deducted at this point and only this point.
{% translate "On the fee." %} The {{ fee_pct|floatformat:"-2" }}% comes out of the payout, not off the top of the client's card. A gig advertised at €400 costs the client €400 — the price on the post is the price they pay, and the worker knows what lands before they accept. If a day ends early, both the pay and the fee are prorated; either side can flag it, and the other has {{ dispute_hours }} hours to disagree before the adjusted amount releases.

{% translate "Two ways to get hired" %}

{% translate "Apply." %} {% translate "The board is public and readable without an account. Find a job, send a message with your application, and the client picks from everyone who put their hand up." %}

{% translate "Be offered it directly." %} {% translate "A client who has found your profile can write a gig for you by name. It never appears on the public board — it goes to you, with a note, and you accept or decline. Declining costs you nothing and needs no reason." %}

{% translate "What we don't do" %}

  • {% translate "We are not your employer." %} {% translate "Work arranged here is between the client and the worker. We hold the money and keep the record." %}
  • {% translate "We do not verify licence numbers." %} {% translate "Trade licences on a profile are self-reported and shown as exactly that. Check with your state board before hiring for regulated work." %}
  • {% translate "We do not judge who was right." %} {% translate "A dispute pauses the automatic release and puts a human in the loop. The timers exist so that neither side can win by going quiet." %}
  • {% translate "We do not use location to block anyone." %} {% translate "If a client adds site coordinates, a check-in records how far away it looked. It is a note on the record, never a condition of checking in — GPS on a building site is unreliable, and a worker who is genuinely there must never be locked out by their handset." %}
